About Sweet Crumbs Bakery

Our story, our team, and our passion for baking

Our Story

Sweet Crumbs Bakery began in 2015 in the kitchen of founder Amara Dlamini. What started as a passion project—baking sourdough loaves for friends and family—quickly grew into something more when word spread about her exceptional bread.

Amara's love affair with baking started during childhood visits to her grandmother's farm in the Free State. She would spend hours watching her ouma knead dough, learning the patience and care that goes into every loaf. Years later, after a career in corporate finance left her unfulfilled, Amara decided to follow her heart.

In 2016, she took a leap of faith and opened a small bakery on 7th Street in Melville. The neighbourhood embraced her immediately. Lines formed before opening time as locals discovered bread that tasted like it came from a European village bakery—because Amara had spent six months training in France before opening.

Today, Sweet Crumbs has grown to two locations—our original Melville home and a bustling kiosk in Sandton City. We've expanded our range to include pastries, custom cakes, and allergen-friendly options, but our philosophy remains unchanged: real ingredients, traditional methods, no shortcuts.

What We Stand For

Our Mission

To bring the joy of authentic, handcrafted baked goods to Johannesburg. We believe everyone deserves access to real bread—made with quality ingredients, time-honoured techniques, and genuine care.

Our Vision

To become South Africa's most trusted artisan bakery, known not just for exceptional products but for the warmth and community we create. We want every customer to feel like family.

Our Values

  • Quality: We never compromise on ingredients or methods
  • Authenticity: Traditional techniques over industrial shortcuts
  • Community: Supporting local suppliers and causes
  • Sustainability: Minimising waste and environmental impact

Meet Our Team

The passionate people behind every loaf, pastry, and cake.

Amara Dlamini

Founder & Head Baker

Amara trained at Le Cordon Bleu in Paris before returning home to start Sweet Crumbs. She still personally crafts every sourdough starter and develops all new recipes. When she's not elbow-deep in flour, she's mentoring young bakers through her community programme.

Sipho Nkosi

Head Pastry Chef

Sipho is the artist behind our stunning celebration cakes and delicate pastries. A graduate of the SA Chefs Academy, he brings creativity and precision to every creation. His specialty? Gravity-defying wedding cakes that taste as good as they look.

Lerato Molefe

Senior Baker

Lerato has been with Sweet Crumbs since day one. She arrives at 3am every morning to start the ovens and manages our bread production. Her ciabatta has won "Best Artisan Bread" at the Joburg Food Festival three years running.

James van der Berg

Operations Manager

James keeps everything running smoothly behind the scenes. With 20 years of hospitality experience, he ensures our customers always receive friendly service. He's also responsible for our popular "Baker for a Day" experience workshops.

Our Journey

Key moments in the Sweet Crumbs story.

  1. 2015

    The Beginning

    Amara Dlamini begins selling sourdough loaves to friends and at the Neighbourgoods Market in Braamfontein.

  2. 2016

    First Bakery Opens

    Sweet Crumbs opens its doors on 7th Street, Melville with just 3 staff members and a single oven.

  3. 2018

    Custom Cake Launch

    Sipho Nkosi joins the team and we launch our custom celebration cake service, which quickly becomes a major part of the business.

  4. 2020

    Surviving the Pandemic

    We pivot to delivery-only during lockdown and launch our first online ordering system. Our customer base grows citywide.

  5. 2022

    Award Winners

    Sweet Crumbs wins "Best Artisan Bakery" at the Eat Out Awards and "Best Sourdough in Gauteng" from the SA Baker's Guild.

  6. 2023

    Sandton City Expansion

    We open our second location—a kiosk in Sandton City Mall—bringing our products to the northern suburbs.

  7. 2024

    Community Programme

    Amara launches the "Young Bakers" programme, offering free training to 12 aspiring bakers from Soweto each year.

  8. 2026

    Today

    Sweet Crumbs serves over 500 customers daily across both locations and employs 28 staff members.
